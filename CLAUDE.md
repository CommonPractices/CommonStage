## ⚠️ DOCTRINE CHANGE 2026-07-27 — North Stars was restructured. Reread before citing it.

**Temporary notice.** Remove it once this project's stated North Stars have been reread and
updated against the current doctrine. It is not a standing rule.

`CommonMind/north-stars-doctrine.md` was rewritten around a **Doctrine-tier axiom set**. What
changed, and why it affects this project's own stated values:

- **Four axioms now bind every project — `Honest → Accessibility → Usability → Choice`** (§2).
  They are the *definition* of a North Star here, not a suggestion. A project does not
  choose whether they bind; it states their **rank** and **reach** for its own surface. A star can
  never be refused (§1.2).
- **`§1.3`'s reality test is REPEALED**, and so is `§3`'s evidence requirement. If this project's
  stated set carries a "where each star forced an outcome" table, **nothing requires it anymore** —
  asking an axiom to justify itself by past outcomes is a category error.
- **`§1.4`'s three forms are GONE** (ranked star / floor outside the ranking / enable-downstream).
  They were longhand for combinations of two variables. A tier now states **rank and reach**, where
  reach is a **partition** — what is in, what is out, and *the condition that draws the line*
  (§2.2). *"Moot, not waived"* survives verbatim.
- **Silence now inherits.** A project that states no ranking inherits the Doctrine-tier one whole.
  The old *"no silent inheritance"* rule is reversed (§3).
- **Any tier may re-rank, uncapped** (§2.3). Re-ranking the axioms wholesale and adding stars of
  its own is exactly what this project is expected to do where its surface warrants.
- **Alternatives are disclosed behind a show/hide reveal, NEVER a feature toggle** (§2.4). A toggle
  makes capability conditionally absent; Accessibility says the obligation goes moot only when a
  modality genuinely does not exist, never when someone hid it.

**Section numbers moved, and one was reused.** A pre-2026-07-27 cite of `§1.3` still resolves and
now points at a *different rule*. Translate stale cites with the `section-ref` rows in
`CommonMind/terminology-migration-doctrine.md` §5 — do not assume a `§` that still resolves is
still correct.

**What this project owes:** reread `north-stars-doctrine.md` §§1–3, then update its own stated set
— the ranking, the reach partitions, and every `§` citation into the doctrine. Per `north-stars-doctrine.md` §3 a North Star change is a **cross-altitude event**: coordinate it
across this repo rather than patching one mention.

**Specific to CommonStage.** Its design spec states accessibility as **ranked star #2, "§1.4 first
form"** — that citation is now dead; §1.4 does not exist. The *ranking* is unaffected; what it owes
is a **reach partition** (§2.2) instead of a form name, and `Honest` ranked rather than omitted.
Its spec lives in this repo at `docs/_working/specs/2026-07-21-commonstage-design.md`.
