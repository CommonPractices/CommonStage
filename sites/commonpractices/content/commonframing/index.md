A blueprint is a language-agnostic description of a recurring product **shape** — its architecture,
its seams, the catalogue of known adapters, and a conformance checklist. It ships **no code you
depend on**; it describes the shape so anyone can build the next instance without re-deriving the
whole thing.

Two blueprints exist today — a debug-channel and a hardware-controller service — both proven against
real builds, with a living logbook of where the shape held and where it leaked.
