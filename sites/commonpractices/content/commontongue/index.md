CommonTongue is the family's shared language: the versioned wire contracts every service and client
speaks, and the per-language packages that realize them. It is the one place in the family that
ships **code you depend on** — so it carries real versions and interface-stability guarantees.

Independent programs interoperate by conforming to these contracts, never by sharing logic or
build-time coupling.
